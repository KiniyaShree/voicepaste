## 📋 What is voicepaste?

voicepaste lets you speak into your microphone and turns your words into text. You can then paste that text into any program like Notepad, Word, or your web browser. It works locally on your computer and does not require you to create any account. You will need your own OpenAI API key to use the speech-to-text feature.

## ▶️ Running voicepaste

1. Find the voicepaste icon on your desktop or in the Start menu.

2. Double-click the icon to open the app.

3. The app will ask for your OpenAI API key. This key allows the app to connect to the speech-to-text service.

4. Enter your API key and save it.

5. Connect your microphone if it is not already connected.

6. Press the shortcut key shown in the app (usually a single key like `F8`) to start speaking.

7. Speak clearly into your microphone.

8. The app will convert your spoken words into text right where your cursor is.

2. Click the **Settings** icon or open the menu and choose **Preferences**.

3. Make changes you want.

## 🛠 How voicepaste Works

voicepaste listens to your voice through your microphone. When you press the shortcut key, it starts recording. Once you stop speaking, voicepaste sends the audio to OpenAI's API for transcription. OpenAI returns the text and voicepaste pastes it wherever your cursor is.

All your data like history, custom words, and settings stay on your computer. Only the audio you speak is sent to OpenAI's servers for transcription.

## 🔧 Troubleshooting

- **voicepaste does not start listening**  
  Check your microphone is connected and not muted. Also, make sure you pressed the correct shortcut key.

- **No text appears after speaking**  
  Check your internet connection. Make sure your OpenAI API key is valid and entered correctly.

- **voicepaste pastes incorrect words**  
  Add difficult words to your dictionary in the settings for better recognition.

- **App crashes or won’t open**  
  Try reinstalling voicepaste. If the problem continues, check for Windows updates and restart your computer.

## 📡 Privacy and Data

voicepaste keeps everything on your local machine except the audio sent to OpenAI’s API. It does not collect personal info or send anything else online. Your speech history and settings save as plain JSON files on your computer in the app’s folder.
